HR Attrition Intelligence

POWER BI / DAX

A five-page Power BI report built on the IBM HR Employee Attrition dataset (1,470 employees), modelling where attrition concentrates and which employee segments it affects. The semantic model carries 34 DAX measures and 11 calculated columns — banded age, tenure, income, promotion and manager groupings, each with explicit sort orders. Pages cover Overview, Demographics, Organization, Career & Tenure, and Compensation & Workload.

  • Pay is the sharpest divide. The lowest income band loses 28.6% of its people against 5.6% in the highest. Leavers average 4,787 a month; those who stay average 6,833.
  • Overtime roughly triples risk. 30.5% attrition among employees working overtime versus 10.4% without — and the gap holds inside every department.
  • Attrition is front-loaded. 39.2% in the 18–24 bracket falling to 9.2% by 45–49, and 27.9% among employees in their first year in a role.
  • Raises are not the lever. Average salary hike sits flat at roughly 15% across every income band, so it does not separate the groups that leave from those that stay.
