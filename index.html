01 / about

Clean data first.
Then dashboards, models and products.

I'm a data analyst and data engineer who likes the unglamorous part: getting messy, multi-source data into a shape people can trust.

I build pipelines that pull from APIs and the web, handle retries, schema drift, gaps and duplicates, and land clean tables in PostgreSQL. On top of that I build Power BI and Tableau dashboards, and forecasting and classification models when the question needs one.

Alongside my BCA at Lovely Professional University, I'm co-inventor on three Indian patent applications and published a paper in IJNRD.
